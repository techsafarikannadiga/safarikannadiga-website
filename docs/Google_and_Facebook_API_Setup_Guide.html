Google

Part 1: Google Reviews API

This lets the website automatically pull in reviews from your Google Business listing.

⚠️ Prerequisite: You must already have a Google Business Profile claimed and verified. If you don't, go to business.google.com first (this takes 1-2 weeks for Google to verify).
1

Find Your Google Place ID

This is a unique code that identifies your business on Google Maps.

  1. Open this link in your browser:
https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/javascript/examples/places-placeid-finder
  1. You'll see a map with a search box at the top
  2. Type your business name (e.g., "SafariKannadiga")
  3. Click on your business from the dropdown
  4. A box will appear showing your Place ID
Your Place ID will look like: ChIJ2eUgeAK6j4ARbn5u_wAGqWA
📋 Copy this ID and paste it somewhere safe. You'll need it later.
2

Create a Google Cloud Account

  1. Go to:
https://console.cloud.google.com
  1. Sign in with the same Google account that owns your Google Business
  2. Click "Agree and Continue" if you see a terms page
3

Create a New Project

  1. At the very top of the page, click the dropdown that says "Select a project"
  2. In the popup window, click "NEW PROJECT" (top right corner)
  3. Type the name: SafariKannadiga
  4. Click "Create"
  5. Wait a few seconds for it to finish
  6. Make sure your new project is selected in the top dropdown
4

Turn On the Places API

  1. Click the ☰ menu (three lines) at the top-left corner
  2. Click "APIs & Services"
  3. Click "Library"
  4. In the search bar, type: Places API
  5. Click on "Places API" from the search results
⚠️ Click "Places API", NOT "Places API (New)" — they are different!
  1. Click the big blue "ENABLE" button
  2. Wait for it to turn on (a few seconds)
5

Create Your API Key

  1. Click the ☰ menu again at the top-left
  2. Click "APIs & Services" → "Credentials"
  3. Click "+ CREATE CREDENTIALS" (blue text at the top)
  4. Select "API key" from the dropdown
  5. A popup will show your new key — COPY IT immediately!
Your API Key will look like: AIzaSyB1234abcdefghijklmnop
🔒 This is like a password! Don't share it publicly. Only send it privately to the developer.
6

Set Up Billing

Google requires a billing account to use their API, but don't worry — you get $200 FREE every month. This website uses less than $2/month, so you won't be charged.

  1. Click ☰ menu → "Billing"
  2. Click "Link a Billing Account" or "Create Account"
  3. Choose your country
  4. Enter a payment method (credit or debit card)
  5. Click "Start Free Trial" or "Submit"
💡 Google's $200/month free credit covers about 40,000 review lookups. Our website does about 30 per day — you'll never come close to being charged.
✅ Done with Google!

You should now have 2 things saved: your Place ID and your API Key.

Facebook / Meta

Part 2: Facebook Reviews API

This lets the website automatically pull in reviews and recommendations from your Facebook Business Page.

⚠️ Prerequisite: You must have a Facebook Business Page (not a personal profile). You must be an admin of that page.
1

Find Your Facebook Page ID

  1. Go to your Facebook Business Page
  2. Click "About" on the left sidebar
  3. Scroll to the very bottom
  4. You'll see "Page ID" — it's a long number
Your Page ID will look like: 123456789012345
💡 Can't find it? Go to findmyfbid.in, paste your Facebook Page URL, and it will show you the Page ID.
2

Create a Meta Developer Account

  1. Open this link:
https://developers.facebook.com
  1. Click "Get Started" or "Log In"
  2. Log in with the same Facebook account that manages your Page
  3. Accept the terms when asked
  4. Choose "Developer" as your role
  5. Verify your account with your phone number or email
3

Create an App

  1. Go to:
https://developers.facebook.com/apps
  1. Click "Create App"
  2. Select "Other" → click "Next"
  3. Select "Business" as app type → click "Next"
  4. Fill in:
    • App Name: SafariKannadiga Website
    • Contact Email: your email
  5. Click "Create App"
  6. Enter your Facebook password to confirm
4

Get Your Access Token

This is the trickiest part — follow carefully!

  1. Open the Graph API Explorer:
https://developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer
  1. In the top-right, where it says "Facebook App", select "SafariKannadiga Website"
  2. Click "Add a Permission" and select:
    • ✅ pages_show_list
    • ✅ pages_read_user_content
  3. Click "Generate Access Token"
  4. A popup will ask you to approve — click "Continue" through all prompts
  5. You now have a temporary token — but it expires in 1 hour!
5

Make the Token Last Forever

  1. Copy the token from Step 4
  2. Open the Access Token Debugger:
https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/accesstoken
  1. Paste your token and click "Debug"
  2. Scroll down and click "Extend Access Token"
  3. A new, longer token will appear — copy it
  4. Now go back to the Graph API Explorer
  5. Paste the extended token into the "Access Token" field
  6. In the URL bar (where it says me?fields=id,name), change it to:
me/accounts
  1. Click "Submit"
  2. In the response, find your page name and copy the "access_token" value next to it
🎉 This is your permanent Page Access Token! It won't expire as long as your page exists.
6

Test That It Works

  1. Still in the Graph API Explorer
  2. Paste your Page Access Token from Step 5
  3. In the URL bar, type:
YOUR_PAGE_ID/ratings?fields=created_time,reviewer,rating,review_text

(Replace YOUR_PAGE_ID with your actual Page ID number from Step 1)

  1. Click "Submit"
  2. If you see review data in the response — it's working! ✅
✅ Done with Facebook!

You should now have 2 things saved: your Page ID and your Page Access Token.

❓ Common Questions

Will Google charge me money?
Almost certainly not. You get $200 free every month. This website uses about $1-2/month total.
Will Facebook charge me?
No. Facebook's API is completely free to use.
My Facebook token stopped working!
If you followed Step 5 correctly (getting the Page token from "me/accounts"), it should be permanent. If it does expire, just repeat Steps 4 and 5 to get a new one.
I can't find my business on the Google Place ID finder
You need a Google Business Profile first. Go to business.google.com to create one. Note: Google takes 1-2 weeks to verify new listings.
Google says "This API project is not authorized"
Make sure you completed Step 4 (enabling the Places API) and Step 6 (setting up billing). Both are required.
I can't find Page ID on my Facebook page
Try going to findmyfbid.in and pasting your page URL. It will show you the ID.
